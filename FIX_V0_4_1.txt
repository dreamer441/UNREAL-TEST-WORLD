Foundation v0.4.1

Fixes a C++/UHT include-order error in EarthTestInputSubsystem.h.
Unreal requires the *.generated.h include to be the final include in every reflected header.
The v0.4 file accidentally included Widgets/SWidget.h after EarthTestInputSubsystem.generated.h.

Install:
1. Close Unreal.
2. Extract this archive into D:\TESTUNREALPROJECT and replace files.
3. Run REBUILD_V0_4_1.bat.
4. Accept the Unreal rebuild prompt.
