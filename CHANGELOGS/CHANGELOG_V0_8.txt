ProjectX / AMADEUS Gameplay v0.8

- Inner Dimension moved out of SpellCreation into a dedicated InnerRealm module.
- TAB now switches the player's view target to a fixed meditation-realm camera.
- Inner realm contains a blank physical plane; the normal gameplay camera/environment are no longer the meditation view.
- SpellCreation is now data-only.
- Shape list simplified to Sphere, Cube, Cone.
- Sphere: radius.
- Cube: X/Y/Z.
- Cone: radius/height.
- Distance, Speed, Mass, Hardness, Toughness, Elasticity retained.
- Earth defaults preserved; Cohesion/Rigidity stay internal.
- EarthMagic replaced fragile procedural spell meshes with stable Unreal BasicShapes behind a shape-realization node.
- Gravity always applies to spawned Earth bodies, including Speed=0 creation.
- E execution remains in disposable EarthTestHarness.
