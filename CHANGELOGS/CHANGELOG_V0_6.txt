ProjectX Foundation v0.6

- Added independent SpellCreation runtime module.
- Added Inner Dimension: TAB switches from physical world to a full-screen blank spell-editing space.
- Moved projectile sliders out of EarthTestHarness and into SpellCreation.
- Spell values persist when returning to the physical world.
- E now casts the stored spell definition.
- EarthTestHarness is execution-only; it no longer owns spell state/UI.
- Fixed debug spam from projectiles bouncing/resting on normal ground.
- Low-speed resting contacts no longer enter the custom material-impact pipeline.
