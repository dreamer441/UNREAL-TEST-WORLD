Foundation v0.4.2

- ImpactSystem no longer maps removed volume to a sphere.
- Added independent shape branch: penetration drive vs lateral spread drive.
- Added crater radius, penetration depth, aspect ratio, and penetration direction to FImpactResult.
- EarthSubtraction now uses an oriented ellipsoid cutter.
- Removed volume remains energy/resistance driven.
- Large/slow projectiles bias wide+shallow; small/fast/dense/hard projectiles bias narrow+deep.
- Test debug line shows radius/depth/shape ratio.
