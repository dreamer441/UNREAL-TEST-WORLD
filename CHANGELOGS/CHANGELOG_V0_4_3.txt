ProjectX Foundation v0.4.3

- Split ImpactSystem internally into three independent calculation nodes:
  Energy, Target Response, Source Response.
- Added explicit impact energy accounting.
- Added projectile/source fracture capacity, integrity, remaining volume,
  residual kinetic energy, residual speed, survival, and continuation result.
- Earth consumes only Target Response and still owns all Earth geometry changes.
- Test projectile consumes only Source Response and can now survive as a smaller
  physical remnant instead of always disappearing.
- High-survival/high-residual-energy projectiles may continue into a sufficiently
  large/deep newly carved channel.
- Debug line now reports target absorbed energy, source integrity, and residual energy.
- No dust/chunk VFX yet; those remain presentation nodes for a later pass.
