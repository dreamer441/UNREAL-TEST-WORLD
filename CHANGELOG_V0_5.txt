ProjectX Foundation v0.5

- Added PhysicalBody runtime module.
- Mass is now a first-class body property; material density remains a material property.
- Test projectile UI now controls mass rather than density and shows derived density.
- Added material Restitution, Toughness and Rigidity.
- Added ContactSolver node.
- Split source structural response from post-impact motion.
- Added MotionSolver for speed loss, rebound/continuation, impulse and estimated average force.
- Gravity is enabled from projectile launch and remains enabled after impact.
- Impact requests use cached pre-impact physics velocity so gravity affects collision calculations.
- Surviving projectiles can collide again after a short anti-double-hit cooldown.
- Non-material collisions use ordinary Unreal rigid-body physics instead of deleting the projectile.
