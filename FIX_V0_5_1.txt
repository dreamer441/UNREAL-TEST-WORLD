ProjectX Foundation v0.5.1 compile fix

Fixes UnrealHeaderTool errors in UE 5.8.2 caused by unsupported Units metadata strings:
- kg/m^3 on DensityKgPerM3
- m^3 on VolumeM3

The fields and calculations are unchanged. Only the editor metadata was removed; the unit semantics remain explicit in the property names and documentation.
